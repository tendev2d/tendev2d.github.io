## Scene 1 — A triggered trap makes Philia name the fear behind survival

**Xem:** `00:00–05:40`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Philia enters a dangerous interior with [Player], a trap closes the route, and the situation recalls a time when she fought alone out of desperation. She later says the frightening part was recognizing what she could do to stay alive, then thanks [Player] for helping her face it.

The scene has two different fears: immediate danger from the trap and a remembered fear of Philia's own survival response. `Be capable of` points to ability, not approval. Her gratitude follows the act of naming that fear, so the retell keeps memory, self-recognition and support in one causal chain without inventing an unseen diagnosis.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
|---|---|---|
| `[PARA]` | `stay alive at all costs` | mục tiêu sinh tồn trong ký ức |
| `[PARA]` | `be capable of fighting` | khả năng khiến Philia sợ |
| `[PARA]` | `more than enough drama` | cách cô đóng cảnh sau khi đã nói ra |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **be capable of + V-ing**: **Philia is frightened by being capable of fighting so desperately.** `Desperation` nói trạng thái bị dồn đến mức hành động, không tự đồng nghĩa với cowardice.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

Trap, survival memory, stated fear and gratitude are source-led. Exact earlier location, enemy count and a permanent mental-health label remain Unknown.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Philia sợ cái bẫy hiện tại hay khả năng sinh tồn cô nhận ra ở chính mình?
2. Sau `capable of` dùng dạng động từ nào?
3. Scene có khóa một diagnosis lâu dài không?

## Scene 2 — A shopping outing becomes a rescue with one hidden hostage

**Xem:** `05:40–17:32`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

In Kirito Mode, Philia proposes shopping for armor and visiting the casino. A robbery interrupts the outing; Kirito and Philia search for hostages, Philia notices an uneven hollow wall, and the last hidden hostage raises the rescue to an S-rank result. A dress/CG beat follows before Philia asks that the day remain their secret.

The scene changes purpose twice: leisure becomes rescue, then public success becomes a private memory. Philia contributes by noticing evidence rather than by receiving a generic compliment. The S rank belongs to the displayed result of this event; it does not make one route, weapon or damage pattern mandatory.
