## Scene 1 — Rivalry gives way to a conversation about different ArFA-sys bonds

**Xem:** `00:00–03:05`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Joe meets [Player], Kureha and ArFA-sys; his competitive manner remains, but the topic shifts from pursuing one AI toward recognizing that ArFA-sys relationships differ.

The scene does not erase Joe's earlier behavior. It changes what the conversation can examine: rivalry is still present, while ArFA-sys stops being treated as a single prize with one interchangeable identity. `Rival` names a person in competition; `rivalry` names the relationship or pattern. The safe retell describes a gradual conversational shift, not a complete moral transformation.

## Scene 2 — A player-made memorial separates remembrance from system requirement

**Xem:** `03:05–07:12`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

The group visits an informal memorial for lost AI; players created the place even though the game system did not require it.

`Memorial` centers remembrance rather than loot, respawn or a developer-authored objective. The passive frame matters because the agent is the contrast: the developers did not create the memorial; players did. Calling the AI `fallen` is a respectful learner paraphrase, not proof that every AI has the same metaphysical status. This scene deepens the topic without turning it into a mandatory quest mechanic.
