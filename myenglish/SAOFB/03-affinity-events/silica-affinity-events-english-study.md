## Scene 1 — Silica confronts a follower and states a clear boundary

**Xem:** `00:00–09:19`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

A female player repeatedly follows and watches Silica around the Glocken. [Player] stays nearby; Silica eventually confronts the follower, asks her to stop, resolves a duel, and chooses forgiveness afterward.

The scene gives Silica agency in stages: she first seeks company and shelter, then names the unwanted behavior, then decides what to do after the immediate threat ends. Forgiveness does not retroactively make stalking acceptable; it comes after a boundary and confrontation. `Keep someone company` describes supportive presence, not speaking in the other person's place.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
|---|---|---|
| `[PARA]` | `keep someone company` | ở bên để người kia không phải đối diện một mình |
| `[PARA]` | `stop stalking` | dừng hành vi bám theo không mong muốn |
| `[PARA]` | `set a boundary` | nói rõ điều không được tiếp tục |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **ask + person + to stop + V-ing**: **Silica asks the follower to stop stalking her.** Sau `stop` là gerund khi nói dừng một hành động.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

Following, fear, company, confrontation and forgiveness are source-led. Exact duel inputs/damage and the follower's full private motive remain player-variable or Unknown.
