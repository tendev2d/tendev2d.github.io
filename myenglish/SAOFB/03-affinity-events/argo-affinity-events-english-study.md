## KHUNG RÁP NGHĨA — khung riêng của bài

Khung của Argo đi từ lời nhờ mapping tới lựa chọn đặt an toàn lên trước: dùng đúng beneficiary, bargain và risk để không biến giao dịch thông tin thành động cơ bí mật.

| # | Khung/cách dùng đã khóa | Câu story đúng để áp dụng | Câu mẫu đã ráp |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | ask + person + to help with + noun | Câu 1: Argo asks [Player] to survey the field and collect elevation data. | Argo asks [Player] to help with field mapping. |
| 2 | refuse to + verb + person | Câu 4: Kirito refuses to abandon Argo, and they escape by diving under the river. | Kirito refuses to abandon Argo during the ambush. |
| 3 | put + noun + first | Câu 6: The second date puts Argo's safety first, but her teasing eventually changes Kirito's mind. | Kirito puts Argo's safety first. |
| 4 | too + adjective + to + verb | Câu 8: Kirito rejects Argo's bluff because their friendship is too important to risk. | Argo is too important to Kirito to risk their friendship. |

### Ba lỗi ráp cần tránh

1. `Ask someone to help with mapping` cần người được nhờ sau `ask`; không dùng `ask help someone with`.
2. `Pay someone back with information` nói cách trả ơn; `pay back information` lại nghe như hoàn trả chính thông tin.
3. `Too important to risk` mang nghĩa không đáng đem ra mạo hiểm; không hiểu sai thành người đó gây ra quá nhiều rủi ro.

## Scene 1 — Field mapping turns information into a form of player safety

**Xem:** `00:00–05:39`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Argo distinguishes field mapping from dungeon mapping, asks [Player] to gather elevation information with the UFG, pays for the result, and explains why information has practical value.

The event begins as business but does not stay at the level of cash. Argo connects terrain knowledge to target acquisition, shooting positions, traps, items and quests. Her payment recognizes surveying as useful work. The closing point about relationships adds another kind of value: an info broker depends on connections as well as data. This does not prove that every map route or reward choice is identical across saves.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
|---|---|---|
| `[PARA]` | `field mapping` | khảo sát ngoài dungeon |
| `[PARA]` | `elevation data` | thông tin về độ cao |
| `[PARA]` | `valuable information` | dữ liệu có thể giúp người khác |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **ask + person + to help with + noun**: **Argo asks [Player] to help with field mapping.** `Survey` mang trọng âm cuối khi là động từ trong cách dùng này.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

Mapping request, UFG/elevation rationale, payment and value-of-information explanation are source-led. Exact survey route, cash/intel reward selection and downstream player outcomes remain route-variable.

### Tự kiểm tra

## Scene 2 — A scanner-backed ambush makes protection a shared escape problem

**Xem:** `05:39–12:24`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Argo asks Kirito for protection at a suspicious information exchange. The contact's group attacks and uses a scanner that searches the map; Kirito refuses to flee alone and proposes removing equipment and diving underwater.

The scene escalates through causes, not random action: an isolated meeting place raises suspicion; the ambush confirms the danger; the scanner closes ordinary hiding routes; the river creates an alternative. Kirito's refusal to abandon Argo gives `protection` a concrete meaning. The successful escape belongs to the scene, but exact combat damage and equipment handling are not canon facts.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
