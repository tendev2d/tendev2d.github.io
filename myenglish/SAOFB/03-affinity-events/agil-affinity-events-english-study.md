## KHUNG RÁP NGHĨA — khung riêng của bài

Khung của Agil tách cho mượn khỏi mượn, suy nghĩ chưa nói khỏi hành động reach out, rồi dùng `be glad + clause` để chốt kết quả mà không dựng reaction giả.

| # | Khung/cách dùng đã khóa | Câu story đúng để áp dụng | Câu mẫu đã ráp |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | lend + object + to + person | Câu 2: His friends model it in battle, and the strange campaign succeeds as a sales stunt. | Agil lends the armor to his friends to advertise it. |
| 2 | have something on + possessive + mind | Câu 4: Agil then admits that an unresolved old friendship has been on his mind. | Agil has an old friendship on his mind. |
| 3 | advise + person + to + verb | Câu 6: Kirito advises him to put the past aside and reach out honestly. | Kirito advises Agil to reach out honestly. |
| 4 | be glad + clause | Câu 8: Rekindling the friendship reminds Agil why shared virtual worlds can matter. | Agil is glad he gave the old friendship another chance. |

### Ba lỗi ráp cần tránh

1. `Lend something to someone` là cho mượn; `borrow something from someone` là mượn từ người khác.
2. `Have something on one's mind` nghĩa là đang bận lòng; không dùng `in one's mind` cho collocation này.
3. Sau `be glad`, dùng `that + clause` hoặc `to + verb`: `Agil is glad he reached out` / `glad to reconnect`.

## Scene 1 — The Champion becomes an exaggerated but effective sales campaign

**Xem:** `00:00–06:17`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Agil gives Kirito, Klein and [Player] first dibs on rare spiked armor called `The Champion`. Because no open-market price exists, the group models it during a hunt to build demand; the sets sell, but Agil's reward is another half-price offer.

The comedy follows a complete business chain: rare stock creates a pricing problem; Klein proposes visible modeling; Agil turns the hunt into an aggressive marketing campaign; the armor sells; the expected profit split becomes another sales pitch. `Sell like hotcakes` describes speed of sales, not the quality of the armor. Stats, exact battle execution and whether the avatar buys a set remain variable.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
|---|---|---|
| `[PARA]` | `first dibs` | được quyền xem/chọn trước |
| `[PARA]` | `drum up demand` | tạo nhu cầu qua màn trình diễn |
| `[PARA]` | `half the going market price` | reward hài hóa thành offer mua hàng |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **lend + object + to + person**: **Agil lends the armor to his friends to advertise it.** `Lend` nhìn từ phía người cho; `borrow` nhìn từ phía người nhận.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

The Champion name, pitch, modeling plan, sold-out result and half-price offer are source-led. Armor stats, damage, exact hunt route and purchase choice are player-variable.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Vì sao Agil không có exact open-market price lúc đầu?
2. Modeling the armor giải quyết vấn đề gì?
3. Reward cuối có phải profit split bằng credits không?

## Scene 2 — A shop compliment opens space for a private worry

**Xem:** `06:17–09:19`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

At Rank 4, the favorable guide response is `Of course. It makes for a great hangout.` Agil appreciates loyal customers, then says a former MMO friend may be in GGO and checks with his wife before sharing more.

`Hangout` reframes the shop as a social place. That relaxed setting contrasts with Agil's unusual restlessness. He does not disclose the whole past immediately; asking his wife first shows that the story involves another person's privacy. The scene therefore supports `have something on one's mind` and a consent boundary, not speculation about the friend.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
|---|---|---|
| `[GUIDE/PARA]` | `great hangout` | nơi tụ họp, không chỉ nơi mua bán |
| `[PARA]` | `have something on his mind` | đang bận lòng |
| `[PARA]` | `check with his wife` | xin phép trước khi kể chuyện chung |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **have something on + possessive + mind**: **Agil has an old friendship on his mind.** Không dùng `in his mind` khi nói một mối lo đang bận tâm.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

Rank/order/response are guide-backed; shop topic, old-friend worry and asking his wife are source-led. The friend's identity and full past remain undisclosed at this point.

### Tự kiểm tra
