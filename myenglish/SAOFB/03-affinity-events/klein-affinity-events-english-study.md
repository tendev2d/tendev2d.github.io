## KHUNG RÁP NGHĨA — khung riêng của bài

Khung của Klein nối quest, công việc và tình bạn: dùng `count on`, `make ends meet` và `part of ... charm` đúng bổ ngữ để không biến lời trêu thành phán quyết về cả con người.

| # | Khung/cách dùng đã khóa | Câu story đúng để áp dụng | Câu mẫu đã ráp |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | keep an eye out for + noun | Câu 1: Klein accepts a Type-B quest to recover ancient combat fatigues. | [Player] keeps an eye out for enemies while Klein mines. |
| 2 | not be cut out to be + noun | Câu 4: In the second event, a failed cool pose reveals that Klein is still the life of the party. | Klein worries that he is not cut out to be the cool, quiet type. |
| 3 | count on + person + to + verb | Câu 5: The first date confirms that Kirito can count on Klein as a dependable friend. | Kirito can count on Klein to support him. |
| 4 | be part of + possessive + charm | Câu 7: The third date accepts Klein's uncertainty as part of his charm. | Klein's uncertainty is part of his charm. |

### Ba lỗi ráp cần tránh

1. `Count on someone to help` cần `to + verb`; `count on someone helping` cũng có thể dùng nhưng là một cấu trúc khác.
2. `Make ends meet` là idiom cố định; không đổi `ends` thành số ít.
3. `Part of someone's charm` chỉ nói một phần; bỏ `part of` sẽ làm nhận định mạnh hơn scene.

## Scene 1 — One Type-B quest carries Klein from a mine to space and back

**Xem:** `00:00–13:30`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Klein accepts a Type-B receptionist's request to recover ancient combat fatigues. With the group watching for danger, he mines a cargo pod, is pulled along when it launches, returns with an outfit design, and learns that the Type-B cannot equip the resulting clothing; Asuna can use the design for crafting. Dialogue/story concludes by 12:29, followed by a 12:29–13:30 save/menu/load tail.

The quest is comic, but its objective chain is precise: request → mining → unexpected launch → return → reward limitation → crafting handoff. The cargo-pod shock and space ride do not create a second affinity event; they are consequences inside the same request. Likewise, save/menu/loading after the outcome are source continuity, not character reaction. Exact mining inputs, enemies, damage and final outfit choice remain variable.

## Scene 2 — A failed cool persona reveals the value of Klein's social energy

**Xem:** `13:30–19:50`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Klein feels discouraged about his appeal and experiments with a cool, quiet persona. The performance does not fit; Agil and the others frame him instead as the life of the party, while ArFA-sys/Lene recognizes his positive, gregarious personality.

The event distinguishes a chosen pose from a trait friends consistently recognize. `Not cut out to be` does not mean Klein can never change; here it means the borrowed cool-and-quiet style clashes with how he naturally keeps conversation moving. The group's laughter and reassurance support a social reading, not a universal judgment about attractiveness or a guaranteed romance result.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
