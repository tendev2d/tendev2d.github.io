### Ba lỗi ráp cần tránh

1. `Treat someone like a younger friend` là so sánh cách đối xử; `treat someone as a child` có thể gán vai trò khác và không nên đổi tùy ý.
2. `Taste test` là noun phrase; khi dùng động từ, nói `taste the food`, không nói `do taste test the food`.
3. `Everyday` là tính từ một từ nghĩa là đời thường; `every day` là trạng ngữ hai từ nghĩa là mỗi ngày.

## Scene 1 — An ordinary day lets Strea try friendship, food, and battle together

**Xem:** `00:00–06:04`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Strea and Kureha discuss always being around each other. Kureha compares her care to an older person looking after someone younger, and Strea asks for an ordinary day together. A taste test reveals Strea's sensitive tongue; the outing reaches a field and a battle before both return to the idea that an ordinary day can still be memorable.

The event does not define normality as inactivity. Food, movement and battle can all belong to an ordinary shared day because the contrast is with obligation or spectacle, not with action itself. Kureha's older-one comparison describes care in this scene; it does not rewrite their official ages or family relationship in the game.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |

| `[PARA]` | `lead a normal life` | chủ đề Strea thử hiểu |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **treat + person + like + noun**: **Kureha treats Strea like a younger friend.** Dùng **what it means to lead a normal life** để nói điều Strea đang khám phá, không phải một định nghĩa tuyệt đối.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

## Scene 2 — Strea turns an arcade visit into a casino performance and private date

**Xem:** `06:04–11:08`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

In Kirito Mode, Strea asks Kirito to go to the arcade because repeated combat feels dull. She leads casino play, adopts a dealer role, and the sequence becomes a more private date/CG. Kirito notices a serious expression amid Strea's playful performance.

The scene distinguishes role-play from emotion. `Place your bets` belongs to the dealer performance, while Kirito's observation marks a change in expression rather than a complete explanation of Strea's thoughts. The private CG is part of the affinity montage, not proof that every casino outcome is fixed.
