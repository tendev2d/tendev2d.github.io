## Scene 1 — A sniper trap turns hesitation into deliberate protection

**Xem:** `00:00–08:43`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Lisbeth, Asuna and Silica encounter a PvP squad while prepared for an enemy hunt. Lisbeth first avoids shooting other players; after a sniper wounds Asuna as bait, she protects her, resolves the threat, and declines to chase the retreating squad.

The scene does not erase Lisbeth's hesitation; it gives that hesitation a boundary. She distinguishes initiating a fight from responding when a teammate is deliberately endangered. Refusing to chase retreaters also matters: protection has an immediate objective, while revenge would extend the conflict after that objective changes. Exact battle execution is not part of the canon claim.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
|---|---|---|
| `[PARA]` | `hesitate to shoot` | do dự trước việc bắn người chơi |
| `[PARA]` | `use someone as bait` | dùng người bị thương để dụ phản ứng |
| `[PARA]` | `refuse to chase retreaters` | dừng khi mối đe dọa rút đi |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **hesitate to + verb**: **Lisbeth hesitates to shoot another player.** Sau `hesitate` dùng infinitive `to shoot`, không dùng bare verb.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

Hesitation, sniper bait, protection and restraint are source-led. Exact weapon, HP, damage, route and whether every enemy is defeated are player-variable.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Điều gì thay đổi Lisbeth từ tránh fight sang bảo vệ Asuna?
2. Vì sao không đuổi retreaters là một ranh giới quan trọng?
3. Scene có khóa damage/weapon không?

## Scene 2 — A group shopping trip makes embarrassment and consent visible

**Xem:** `08:43–14:03`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Yui joins Lisbeth, Silica, Sinon, Zeliska and Daisy during swimsuit shopping. The group discusses appearance and attraction, while the girls prevent Kirito from seeing before they are comfortable.

The learning point is not the swimsuit itself. `Try on` names a private test of clothing; `feel uncomfortable` marks a person's response; stopping an observer preserves consent around when an appearance is shared. Teasing can reveal group familiarity, but it cannot settle a single romance label for every participant.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
|---|---|---|
| `[PARA]` | `try on a swimsuit` | mặc thử trước khi quyết định |
| `[PARA]` | `feel uncomfortable` | không sẵn sàng để người khác nhìn |
| `[PARA]` | `respect a boundary` | dừng lại khi người khác chưa đồng ý |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **try on + clothing**: **They try on swimsuits before showing them to anyone.** `Try` alone does not state clothing is worn.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

Shopping group, embarrassment and access boundary are source-led. Exact romantic feelings, preferred outfit and purchase result remain person-/route-variable.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. `Try on` thêm nghĩa gì so với `try`?
2. Việc ngăn Kirito nhìn khóa ranh giới nào?
3. Group teasing có tự chứng minh romance label không?
