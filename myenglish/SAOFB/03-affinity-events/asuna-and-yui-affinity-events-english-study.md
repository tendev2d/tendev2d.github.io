## Scene 1 — Outfit preparation becomes care for the person who will wear it

**Xem:** `A 00:00–04:18`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

In Asuna's first Normal event, she checks how [Player] is adapting and connects GGO preparation with looking after companions; the outfit quest is an unlock prerequisite.

The scene treats crafting as relational work rather than a detached material checklist. `Make an outfit for someone` marks the beneficiary; `look after someone` marks care, not physical surveillance. ## Scene 2 — A personal touch gives crafted equipment meaning beyond stats

**Xem:** `A 04:18–08:24`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

Asuna discusses tailoring and the maker's personal touch; the Rank 3 Normal event includes its CG payoff according to the unlock ledger.

`Personal touch` means a recognizable contribution from the maker, not a touchscreen action. The equipment still has gameplay statistics, but the scene asks the learner to name a second layer: time, attention and authorship. A CG records route payoff; it does not become a separate event or prove one canon outfit appearance for every save. The retell therefore contrasts meaning with a stat sheet.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng
