3. `Be like a partner to someone` là phép so sánh; không bỏ `like` nếu scene chỉ nói sự tương đồng.

## Scene 1 — Distance and accuracy become safeguards against overconfidence

**Xem:** `00:00–04:44`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

In the Normal Rank 3 event, Sinon discusses aiming, maintaining distance and refusing to let accuracy turn into overconfidence.

Accuracy is a result measure, not permission to ignore positioning. `Keep one's distance from` names separation relative to a target; `line of sight` names whether the shot path is open. The scene links confidence to discipline: good aim helps, but overconfidence can make a player abandon cover or range. Retell sentences one and two preserve the tactical contrast without prescribing an exact weapon or distance number.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
|---|---|---|
| `[PARA]` | `keep a safe distance` | giữ cự ly an toàn |
| `[PARA]` | `accuracy` | độ chính xác |
| `[PARA]` | `not become overconfident` | không biến kỹ năng thành chủ quan |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **[Player] keeps a safe distance from the target.** `Distance from` identifies the reference point; `overconfident` takes no hyphen in this spelling.

## Scene 2 — Experience manages fear without pretending to erase it

**Xem:** `04:44–08:17`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

In the Kirito-Mode event, Sinon and Kirito discuss tension in GGO; experience helps control fear, while fear does not simply disappear.

`Control fear` means keep it manageable enough to act; `erase fear` would claim complete removal. The contrast is psychologically and grammatically important. A calm manner can coexist with tension, so outward composure is not proof of an empty emotional state. The lesson does not import Death Gun plot facts here; it stays inside the affinity conversation's narrower claim about experience and fear.

### Móc nhận diện

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng **Experience does not erase fear; instead, it helps control it.** `Instead` introduces the corrected alternative, not an additional identical action.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

## Scene 3 — Date one treats shared danger as thrilling rather than careless

**Xem:** `08:17–13:14`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

At Rank 4, the favorable guide response is `Yeah, it's pretty thrilling,` when the pair discuss the excitement of battle or shared activity.

`Thrilling` describes intense positive excitement; it does not mean `dangerous` by itself. `Pretty` is an informal degree adverb here, not the adjective `beautiful`. The date accepts adrenaline as part of GGO while earlier range discipline still prevents excitement from becoming recklessness. The response is route-specific, so alternate feelings remain possible.
