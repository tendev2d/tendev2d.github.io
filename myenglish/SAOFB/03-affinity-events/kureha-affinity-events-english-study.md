## Scene 2 — A group quest gives comparison fatigue a name

**Xem:** `05:58–11:52`

During a shared quest with Kirito and Leafa, Kureha confronts the strain of comparing herself with people she sees as more capable.

`Live in someone's shadow` describes identity being measured through another person. `Second-rate` is an evaluative label, not an objective stat. The value of the scene is the admission: Kureha can say the comparison is exhausting instead of disguising it as pure competition.
