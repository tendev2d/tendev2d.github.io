## Beat 2 — The uncancelled dark charge closes the normal route's combat window

**Xem:** `20:42–22:49`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

The GM completes the dark-sphere charge and the normal-ending crisis triggers; this recorded route does not down the boss during the required cancellation window.

The guide separates prerequisite from execution: the ArFA-sys Charm permits the beam to be cancelled, but the player still has to down The GM during each charge. Footage locks the uncancelled result; it does not prove whether the save lacked the charm or the run missed the down window. Build, weak-point method and damage remain player-variable.

### Móc nhận diện

| Nhãn | Cụm ngắn | Bắt ý |
|---|---|---|
| `[PARA]` | `complete a charged attack` | hoàn tất đòn đang tích |
| `[GUIDE/PARA]` | `down the boss during the charge` | làm boss ngã trong cửa sổ tích lực |
| `[PARA]` | `trigger the normal-ending crisis` | kích hoạt khủng hoảng ending thường |

### Ngôn ngữ dùng ở beat này

Dùng `fail to cancel + attack` cho result; chỉ dùng `because` khi source khóa nguyên nhân của save.

### Ranh giới bằng chứng

### Tự kiểm tra
