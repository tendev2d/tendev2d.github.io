## Nguồn, phạm vi và cách dùng bài

### Source and study scope

- **Direct source A:** [Sword Art Online: Fatal Bullet - Walkthrough Part 2 Kureha](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M7kwDL_x8X4) — BlazzerSora; PlayStation 4; English UI/text; no commentary; **43:01**.
- **Direct source B:** [Sword Art Online: Fatal Bullet - Walkthrough Part 3 Kirito & Friends](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n8VEvMHHXFI) — same uploader, platform, language, and presentation; **35:29**.
- **Study clocks:** source A **36:45–43:01**, followed by source B **00:00–35:29**.
- **Title check:** the active quest HUD displays **The SBC Glocken**. Its meet-and-tour objective was cross-checked against the [GameFAQs walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/217400-sword-art-online-fatal-bullet/faqs/75678/walkthrough).
- **Name policy:** `[Player]` and `[ArFA-sys]` replace custom avatar names. The lesson avoids gendered references to either.

### Localization and uncertainty flags

- **Source-boundary flag:** the uploader splits the quest between Parts 2 and 3. Each listed clock is internally continuous, and the upload cut is shown openly.
- Source A begins on the previous quest's completion frame because Kirito's proposal follows immediately; it is kept as the narrative handoff rather than silently dropped.
- Some city meetings can be approached in a slightly different order during play. This lesson follows the recorded route, not a claim that every save must use that order.
- Character lines are paraphrased. Only short anchors visible in the English recording, such as **“PvP play”** and **“one of your friends,”** are retained.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Ranh giới bằng chứng tiếng Anh:** video có English UI hoặc hard-sub trong phạm vi nguồn để đối chiếu tên hiển thị, biên và các cụm ngắn; câu kể nối mạch vẫn là `[PARA]`/`[INFERENCE]`.
>
> `[SUB/UI]` chỉ dành cho nhãn hoặc móc rất ngắn. `[PARA]` là câu luyện kể; `[PLAYER-VARIABLE]` là build/damage/route lựa chọn; `[UNKNOWN]` giữ nguyên phần nguồn chưa khóa.

### Mục tiêu sử dụng

Người học cần đi từ opening **“Kirito leads [Player] from the introductions toward the Governor's Office.”** tới outcome **“Back at home, the group forms a squad to pursue the SBC Flügel.”**, đồng thời dùng được các chunk chính của `The SBC Glocken` mà không đổi guide/gameplay thành canon dialogue.

3. `complete an objective` nhưng `complete a quest`; không dùng damage number để chứng minh story.
