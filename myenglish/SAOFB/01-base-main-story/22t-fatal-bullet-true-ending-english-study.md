## Beat 2 — Downing The GM during each dark charge cancels the normal-ending trigger

**Xem:** `00:34–04:18`

### Mạch story, reaction và logic

The party fights The GM and downs it during the dangerous charge windows, cancelling the lethal beam before defeating the first form.

The guide is precise: the charm allows cancellation, but the player must still down the boss when it charges, including the later charge. Damage is only the means; the required state change is boss down before the beam fires. Weapon, weak-point choice, party and clear time remain player-variable.

### Móc nhận diện
