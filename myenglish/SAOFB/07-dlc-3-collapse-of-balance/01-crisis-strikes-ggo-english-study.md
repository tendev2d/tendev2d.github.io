## KHUNG RÁP NGHĨA — khung riêng của bài

| # | Khung/cách dùng đã khóa | Câu story đúng để áp dụng | Câu mẫu đã ráp |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | descend upon + place | Câu 2: Enemy ArFA-sys units descend on the city and keep players from moving around freely. | Enemy ArFA-sys units descend upon SBC Glocken. |
| 2 | keep + person + from + gerund | Câu 2: Enemy ArFA-sys units descend on the city and keep players from moving around freely. | The swarm keeps players from moving around freely. |
| 3 | mistake + noun + for + noun | Câu 5: Many players mistake the attack for a harmless surprise event and remain on the sidelines. | Many players mistake the attack for a surprise event. |
| 4 | stay behind to + verb + and buy + person + time | Câu 8: Zeliska's group goes after Lievre while Kirito stays behind to buy them time. | Kirito stays behind to defend the city and buy the others time. |

### Ba lỗi ráp cần tránh

1. Dùng `descend upon the city`; `descend` có thể đứng một mình, nhưng khung này cần `upon` trước địa điểm bị ập tới.
2. Dùng `keep players from moving`; sau `from` là gerund, không dùng `from move`.
3. Dùng `mistake the attack for an event`, không dùng `mistake the attack as an event` trong collocation này.
