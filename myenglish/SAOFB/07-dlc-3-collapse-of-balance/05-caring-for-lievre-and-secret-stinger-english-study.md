## KHUNG RÁP NGHĨA — khung riêng của bài

Bốn khung giữ paid prerequisite, capsule problem, omitted collection và stable-but-not-awake outcome theo đúng thứ tự. Counts/reward vẫn mang vai trò guide; stinger chỉ foreshadow chứ không chứng minh unlock.

| # | Khung/cách dùng đã khóa | Câu story đúng để áp dụng | Câu mẫu đã ráp |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | choose + option + and pay + amount + for + noun | Câu 1: In the selected paid branch, the player gives Argo thirty million credits for information about Lievre. | The player chooses the paid option and gives Argo thirty million credits for information. |
| 2 | prevent + object + from + V-ing + at full capacity | Câu 2: Yui explains that missing parts keep Lievre's capsule below full repair capacity. | Missing parts prevent the capsule from repairing Lievre at full capacity. |
| 3 | after + noun/gerund, subject + verb | Câu 4: After the omitted field phase, the player selects Give Materials and hands the items over. | After the omitted field phase, the player selects Give Materials. |
| 4 | although + clause, subject + confirm + that-clause | Câu 6: Lievre does not wake immediately, but Yui confirms that her condition is stable. | Although Lievre does not wake, Yui confirms that her condition is stable. |

### Ba lỗi ráp cần tránh

1. Dùng `ask someone a favor` không cần `for`: `Yui asks the player a favor`; hoặc dùng `ask someone for help`.
2. Dùng `prevent the capsule from operating at full capacity`; sau `prevent` phải có `from + V-ing`.
3. Dùng `Although Lievre does not wake, her condition is stable`; không viết comma splice giữa hai mệnh đề độc lập.
