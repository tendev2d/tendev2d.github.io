## KHUNG RÁP NGHĨA — khung riêng của bài

Bốn khung giữ unlock theo save, Alice destination, giới hạn footage Eugeo và conflict LLENN thành các bài đọc riêng. Không khâu ba upload thành một story clock hoặc dùng generic Results/menu/map để giả reward.

| # | Khung/cách dùng đã khóa | Câu story đúng để áp dụng | Câu mẫu đã ráp |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | become available after + progress | Câu 1: The three character combat requests become available after the relevant DLC3 progress, depending on the save. | The requests become available after the relevant DLC3 progress. |
| 2 | send + person + toward + place | Câu 2: Alice's video begins in the lobby and sends the player toward Lost Gate Depths. | Alice's request sends the player toward Lost Gate Depths. |
| 3 | show + object + but not + missing evidence | Câu 5: Eugeo's field footage identifies Enhanced Abyss Guide but shows no request completion or reward. | Eugeo's video shows the target but not a request completion or reward. |
| 4 | when + plural noun + conflict, keep + noun + unresolved | Câu 8: When the LLENN repeat rows conflict, the exact repeat target and reward must remain unresolved. | When the LLENN repeat rows conflict, keep the exact target unresolved. |

### Ba lỗi ráp cần tránh

1. Dùng `become available after the relevant progress`; adjective `available` không nhận tân ngữ trực tiếp.
2. Dùng `the video shows the target but not the reward`; `but not` giữ hai phạm vi bằng chứng song song.
