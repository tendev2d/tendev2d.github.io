## Beat 1 — Rhondson announces the coming departure and the quest begins

**Xem:** `00:15–01:14`

### Mạch story

Rhondson introduces a family change that Tarrey Town cannot solve with construction: Mattison will soon leave for Gerudo Town. The scene establishes departure as a normal Gerudo family tradition rather than a punishment. Hudson and Rhondson both care about their daughter, yet they respond differently to the same future. Rhondson speaks with practical acceptance while Hudson is visibly weighed down by it.

The Adventure Log title appears in this opening stretch, so the lesson can treat `Mattison's Independence` as exact UI. The title names Mattison's growing ability to act on her own; it does not mean that the family relationship is ending. Cảnh chỉ xác nhận rằng departure is approaching và cha mẹ cần hỗ trợ để chuẩn bị.

The emotional cause chain begins here: the move creates uncertainty, uncertainty makes Hudson worry, and that worry creates an opportunity for Link to help. No railcar, granny question, Sundelion count, or reward belongs inside this first scene. Those later states must remain outside this scene so the learner can recognize what the opening actually establishes.

   **Đọc Việt:** Mattison lii-v-z uị(đh) CON-phờ-đần-x, en(d) đờ kwe-x(t) ờ-UO(r)D-z tuu XI-l-vờ(r) RUU-pii-z.

### Retell 60 giây

Kể tám câu theo nhịp `departure → request → lesson plan → Vaba → railcar → ten Sundelions → balloon flight → sendoff/reward`. Nếu một câu gắn một obstruction material bắt buộc, sửa lại bằng `can` hoặc `one workable method`.
