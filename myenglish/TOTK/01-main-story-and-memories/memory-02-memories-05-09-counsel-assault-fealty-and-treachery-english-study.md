## KHUNG RÁP NGHĨA

| Mục đích | Khung English | Ví dụ đúng phạm vi |
|---|---|---|
| Report evidence | `The device supports ___, but it does not prove ___.` | `The Purah Pad supports Zelda's time-travel account, but it does not prove a return route.` |
| Explain a price | `The method may ___, yet it would cost ___.` | `The method may reach the future, yet it would cost Zelda her sense of self.` |
| Track cause and observation | `After ___ repels ___, ___ notices ___.` | `After Rauru repels the Moldugas, Ganondorf notices the Secret Stone.` |
| Separate words from motive | `___ pledges ___ while concealing ___.` | `Ganondorf pledges fealty while concealing hostile intent.` |
| Describe a demonstrated power | `Sonia reverses ___, showing that ___.` | `Sonia reverses the object's motion, showing how Recall follows memory and movement.` |
| Hold an entry line | `This entry ends with ___; ___ belongs to the next entry.` | `This entry ends with Sonia falling; the visible stone theft belongs to Memory 10.` |
