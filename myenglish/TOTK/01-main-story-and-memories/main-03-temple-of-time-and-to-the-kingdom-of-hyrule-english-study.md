## KHUNG RÁP NGHĨA

- `After leaving A, Link reaches B`: kể chuyển địa điểm, không buộc route ở giữa.
- `The quest completes when + state`: gắn completion với checkpoint thấy được.
- `A new objective sends Link to + person/place`: mô tả Adventure Log direction.

- `Because A reports B, C becomes more urgent`: dùng causal link khi phản ứng nhân vật có bằng chứng.
- `Between these windows, the player may + action`: đánh nhãn khoảng traversal mở.

## Beat 1 — Link dives from Great Sky Island to the Surface

**Xem:** `01:56:10–01:57:40`

### Mạch story

With the Temple of Time path open behind him, Link runs toward the edge of Great Sky Island and jumps. The camera follows a long vertical dive through clouds. The sky environment recedes above while water and land on the Surface become the immediate destination below. This is a deliberate transition between two world layers, not merely another fall inside the island tutorial.

Link controls the descent and lands in water, establishing a safe arrival on the Surface in this walkthrough. The regional title identifies Hyrule Kingdom. That title card is a geographic confirmation: the journey named `To the Kingdom of Hyrule` has reached its world layer, although the quest still requires Link to report at Lookout Landing.

The direct consequence is a new navigation scale. Great Sky Island no longer limits the route, and the kingdom opens around Link. The exact direction, pickups and stops after the landing belong to player play. The story fact locked here is narrower and stronger: he dives through the clouds, survives the landing, and begins the Surface leg of the search for Zelda.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình:** Great Sky Island edge, dive posture, cloud layer, water landing, Hyrule Kingdom title.
- **Phrase:** `dive through the clouds` belongs here, not in the prior Great Sky Island beats.
- **Boundary:** landing place in this walkthrough does not make every legal player landing identical.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `descend from one world layer to another`: hạ từ một tầng thế giới sang tầng khác.
- `land safely in water`: đáp an toàn xuống nước.
- Contrast `fall` /fɔl/ (không nêu kiểm soát) with `dive` /daɪv/ (hành động có hướng).

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Link commits to the descent after receiving the search direction.
- **Logic:** the dive causes the world-layer transition; it does not itself complete the reporting objective.
- **Player-variable:** steering, exact landing point and actions immediately after landing.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Ba visual cue nào chứng minh Link chuyển từ Sky sang Surface?
2. Title card xác nhận geography nào nhưng chưa xác nhận quest completion nào?
3. Vì sao `dive` chính xác hơn `fall` khi kể hành động của Link ở cửa sổ này?

## Beat 2 — Lookout Landing becomes the reporting destination

**Xem:** `02:37:20–02:40:40`

### Mạch story

The walkthrough returns to the main objective after a broad stretch of player-controlled travel. Link approaches a defended settlement whose walls, tents, lookout structures and central activity distinguish it from the ruined landscape outside. The location is Lookout Landing, the new coordination hub built near Hyrule Castle after the Upheaval.

Arrival does not mean Link has already spoken to Purah. This beat locks the transition from open field to hub and the search for the correct contact inside. NPC placement and signage help orient the player. The direct route used by BeardBear is evidence for one successful approach, but it is not a canon requirement that every player copy the same road or collect the same objects.

The consequence is organizational rather than dramatic: Link now has access to the settlement where his report can be received. The larger objective narrows from “reach the kingdom” to “find Purah within Lookout Landing.” The beat ends at the hub state; Josha/Purah's substantive response is kept for the next clock so arrival and briefing do not collapse into one oversized scene.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình:** settlement walls/gate, tents and central operations at Lookout Landing.
- **State:** open-field traversal ends; hub navigation begins.
- **Do not claim:** one mandatory Surface route from landing to the gate.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `a coordination hub`: trung tâm điều phối.
- `enter a defended settlement`: đi vào khu định cư có phòng thủ.
- Contrast `arrive at` /əˈraɪv æt/ with `report to` /rɪˈpɔrt tu/: đến nơi trước, gặp người sau.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Link searches within the hub rather than continuing directly to the castle.
- **Logic:** the quest direction makes Lookout Landing relevant; arrival enables the report but does not equal report completion.
- **Player-variable:** road, stops, optional encounters and time spent before entering.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Những landmark nào giúp nhận diện Lookout Landing như hub?
2. Tại sao “arrived at Lookout Landing” và “reported to Purah” phải là hai state?
3. Viết một câu giữ route trước cổng ở nhãn Player-variable.

## Beat 3 — Purah receives the report and opens the castle crisis

**Xem:** `02:45:50–02:52:10`

### Mạch story

Inside Lookout Landing, Link locates Josha and then Purah. Their reactions reconnect the isolated Great Sky Island experience with the kingdom's current crisis. Link reports the damaged arm, Zelda's disappearance and his return. The people at the hub now know he survived, but this meeting does not supply a confirmed location for Zelda.

The UI records a major state change. `To the Kingdom of Hyrule` completes because Link has reached the hub and made contact. Character Profiles become available, giving the player structured information about known figures. Purah then starts `Crisis at Hyrule Castle`, turning the broad search into a concrete instruction to contact Captain Hoz and his search party near the castle.

Purah's response provides causality: because Link's report concerns the castle, Zelda and the Upheaval, she directs him toward the team already investigating that area. Completion and opening should not be merged. One quest closes as a travel/report objective; the next begins as an investigation. The consequence is a new active marker and a named contact, not proof that the crisis can be solved immediately.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình/UI:** Josha, Purah, quest-complete state, Character Profiles unlock, Crisis at Hyrule Castle start.
- **Objective:** contact Captain Hoz/search party.
- **Boundary:** Purah receives a report; she does not verify Zelda's location.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `receive a report`: nhận báo cáo.
- `complete one quest and begin another`: hoàn tất một quest rồi mở quest khác.
- Contrast `profile` /ˈproʊfaɪl/ with `proof` /pruf/: hồ sơ tổ chức thông tin, không tự là bằng chứng cuối.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Purah moves from relief/briefing to an actionable castle assignment.
- **Logic:** Link's information + existing search party explains why Hoz is the next contact.
- **Consequence:** To the Kingdom complete; Crisis active; Character Profiles available.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Quest nào complete và quest nào begins trong cùng cửa sổ?
2. Character Profiles unlock có vai trò thông tin gì, và không chứng minh điều gì?
3. Người nào được Purah chỉ định làm contact kế tiếp?

## Beat 4 — Link reaches Captain Hoz's search party

**Xem:** `03:01:00–03:04:40`

### Mạch story

After leaving the hub, Link heads toward Hyrule Castle and the search party. In the walkthrough, an optional Kyononis Shrine detour appears before the locked window. That detour can help the player, but it is not part of the required causal chain. The story resumes as Link crosses the castle approach and passes recognizable ruins and the First Gatehouse area.

The raised castle dominates the background, giving the objective a visible location. Guards and expedition members indicate that Link has reached the organized search rather than wandering alone into the ruins. He finds Captain Hoz among the team. Hoz's presence is the verification cue for Purah's instruction “contact Hoz,” while route variations before the meeting remain open.

Hoz reacts to Link's arrival with recognition and relief before attention shifts upward. The beat's consequence is completion of the contact step, not yet the sighting itself. Separating these states matters: reaching Hoz is an objective result; what Hoz and the team see moments later becomes new evidence that changes the investigation.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình:** castle approach, First Gatehouse area, search-party guards, Captain Hoz, raised Hyrule Castle.
- **Route boundary:** optional Kyononis is excluded from the required narrative chain.
- **Objective verify:** Link is physically with Hoz's team before the distant appearance.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `contact the search party`: liên lạc đội tìm kiếm.
- `pass a landmark`: đi qua một mốc địa hình.
- Contrast `reach Hoz` with `reach Zelda`: the former is achieved; the latter is not.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Hoz acknowledges Link, then the group's attention moves toward the castle.
- **Logic:** Purah's marker leads to a named contact. Contact enables the next observation but does not cause Zelda's appearance.
- **Player-variable:** route, detours, combat and supplies on the castle approach.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Landmark và NPC cue nào xác nhận Link đã tới đúng search party?
2. Vì sao optional Kyononis Shrine không được kể như bước bắt buộc của Crisis?
3. Viết một câu phân biệt `contact Hoz` với `find Zelda`.

## Beat 5 — A distant Zelda appears and vanishes above the castle

**Xem:** `03:04:40–03:06:50`

### Mạch story

While Link stands with Hoz's party, their attention turns toward the raised Hyrule Castle. A distant figure resembling Zelda appears high above them. The visual association is strong enough for the characters to react as if they may be seeing the missing princess, but distance prevents close verification. The wording “a Zelda-like figure” preserves that evidence boundary.

Before anyone can reach the figure, the image vanishes in a bright effect. Appearance and disappearance are both observed states; neither explains motive, safety or identity. Hoz cannot convert the sighting into a rescue. Instead, he treats it as urgent information and tells Link to return to Purah, who can coordinate the next response.

The logic is sequence plus reaction, not an unsupported causal theory. The source shows figure appears → party watches → figure vanishes → Hoz sends report back. It does not show why she appeared, whether she consciously called Link or where she went. The consequence is a high-priority but unresolved lead attached to Hyrule Castle.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình:** tiny/distant Zelda-like silhouette over raised castle, watchers below, flash of light, empty space afterward.
- **Evidence language:** `resembles Zelda`, `appears`, `vanishes`; avoid `is rescued` or `returns home`.
- **Character response:** Hoz sends Link back to Purah.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `a distant figure resembling Zelda`: hình ở xa giống Zelda.
- `vanish in a flash of light`: biến mất trong chớp sáng.
- Contrast `sighting` /ˈsaɪtɪŋ/ with `identification` /aɪˌdɛntəfəˈkeɪʃən/.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Hoz treats the event as report-worthy and redirects Link to the command hub.
- **Logic:** sighting increases urgency; it does not prove identity or explain disappearance.
- **Unknown:** who/what the figure is, why it appears, and its destination.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Hai visual state liên tiếp nào tạo thành sighting?
2. Hoz phản ứng bằng objective gì thay vì tổ chức rescue ngay tại chỗ?
3. Tại sao `resembles Zelda` chuẩn hơn `is confirmed as Zelda`?

## Beat 6 — Purah receives the sighting and keeps the crisis open

**Xem:** `03:08:10–03:10:30`

### Mạch story

Link returns from the castle approach to Lookout Landing and reports what the search party saw. The hub structure now matters: Hoz gathers field evidence, while Purah receives it and decides how the investigation should proceed. Link's return closes the immediate report loop without closing `Crisis at Hyrule Castle`.

Purah responds to the apparent Zelda sighting as an urgent clue. She has to balance two facts: multiple witnesses saw a figure, yet the figure vanished before direct contact. Her analysis therefore remains conditional. She does not announce that Zelda has been found, nor does the Adventure Log mark the whole crisis complete.

The beat ends at a checkpoint rather than a resolution. The castle remains raised, Zelda's whereabouts remain unknown and Lookout Landing remains the reporting center. This unresolved state prepares the next lesson: Purah must finish the Skyview Tower setup, obtain a broader map and redirect Link toward reports from four regions.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình:** return to Lookout Landing, Purah briefing/report reception, active Crisis state.
- **Report chain:** Hoz observes → Link carries report → Purah evaluates.
- **Boundary:** the lead is urgent but unconfirmed; increased urgency is a reaction, còn confirmed Zelda location không phải kết quả.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `report an unconfirmed sighting`: báo lần nhìn thấy chưa xác minh.
- `keep the investigation open`: giữ điều tra tiếp tục.
- Contrast `urgent` /ˈɝdʒənt/ with `certain` /ˈsɝtən/: khẩn cấp không đồng nghĩa chắc chắn.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Purah accepts the report and plans the next survey/investigation step.
- **Logic:** Hoz's observation motivates the return; it does not settle the identity question.
- **Consequence:** Crisis advances while Zelda remains missing.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Chuỗi ba vai Hoz → Link → Purah vận chuyển loại thông tin nào?
2. Quest Crisis ở trạng thái complete hay active sau báo cáo?
3. Viết một câu dùng `urgent but unconfirmed` cho lần thấy Zelda.

### Test 0 — Truy hồi có ranh giới

Đánh dấu fixed visual state hoặc Player-variable: dive, water landing, every road pickup, Lookout Landing arrival, Kyononis completion, meeting Hoz, figure vanishing.
