## KHUNG RÁP NGHĨA

- `The report suggests X, but does not confirm Y`: tách manh mối và kết luận.
- `While A is being prepared, Link can B`: mô tả khoảng chuẩn bị mà không biến hoạt động tùy chọn thành canon.
- `After the network activates, the terminal can + verb`: nối system prerequisite.
- `The tower launches Link so the device can scan + layers`: kể purpose thấy rõ.
- `There are four reports in + regions`: tập số nhiều phenomena/reports.
- `The order is open, although + lead + is emphasized`: giữ lựa chọn người chơi và ưu tiên story cùng lúc.

## Beat 1 — Purah weighs the castle sighting without confirming Zelda

**Xem:** `03:12:20–03:14:40`

### Mạch story

After receiving Link's report from Captain Hoz, Purah turns the distant appearance into an investigation problem. She treats the sighting as urgent because it occurred above the raised Hyrule Castle, the same area tied to the Upheaval and Zelda's disappearance. Multiple witnesses strengthen the report that a figure appeared; distance and disappearance weaken any confident identification.

Purah's reaction is analytical rather than celebratory. The new evidence does not let her say Zelda is safe or reachable. Instead, she considers the castle crisis still active and looks for a way to broaden the search. This is why the lesson uses `urgent but unconfirmed`: urgency determines what the team does next, while uncertainty limits what they may conclude.

The consequence is a shift from a narrow field sighting to survey preparation. Purah's Skyview Tower work becomes relevant because a map and wider view can support movement across Hyrule. The source shows this operational transition; it does not provide a canon speech transcript for the learner's English. Exact report wording remains outside the locked evidence.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình:** Purah at Lookout Landing, discussion after Link's return, raised-castle context.
- **Evidence pair:** figure sighting is observed; identity/location remain unconfirmed.
- **Next-state cue:** attention turns from castle report to tower/survey preparation.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `assess an urgent lead`: đánh giá manh mối cần hành động nhanh.
- `keep the identity unconfirmed`: không nâng sighting thành identification.
- Contrast `urgent` /ˈɝdʒənt/ and `certain` /ˈsɝtən/.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Purah takes the report seriously and prepares a broader information system.
- **Logic:** the sighting motivates more investigation; it does not cause or explain the Upheaval.
- **Unknown:** identity, motive and destination of the castle figure.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Vì sao witness report tăng urgency nhưng không đủ xác nhận identity?
2. Purah chuyển sang chuẩn bị hệ thống nào để mở rộng khảo sát?
3. Viết một câu dùng `may connect to` mà không đổi thành `proves`.

## Beat 2 — Final adjustments create a preparation checkpoint

**Xem:** `03:14:40–03:18:50`

### Mạch story

Purah indicates that the Skyview Tower still needs final adjustments before it can be used. Instead of launching immediately, Link receives a short preparation window around Lookout Landing. The Emergency Shelter becomes a concrete service point where he can rest and learn about the settlement's support structure while the technical work is completed.

This gap must be described carefully. The shelter and waiting state are story-supported, but each player's meal, inventory management, conversation order and time spent are not fixed. One player may rest at once; another may inspect the hub. The lesson therefore locks “prepare while the tower is unfinished” rather than inventing a mandatory shopping or cooking sequence.

The consequence is readiness on both sides. Purah's team finishes the system work, while Link has access to a safe place before the survey. When he is directed back toward the tower, the state has changed from unavailable to ready for activation. The next beat begins with the visible network event, not with an assumed off-screen technical explanation.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình:** Lookout Landing services, Emergency Shelter entrance/interior, return direction to tower.
- **State:** tower not yet ready → preparation window → meet Purah when ready.
- **Boundary:** exact rest/food/equipment choices remain Player-variable.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `final adjustments`: những điều chỉnh cuối trước khi vận hành.
- `rest and prepare`: nghỉ và chuẩn bị.
- Contrast `wait for` /weɪt fɔr/ with `waste time`: waiting has an explicit system prerequisite.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Link uses the hub checkpoint while Purah's team completes tower work.
- **Logic:** unfinished adjustment explains delayed launch; no source supports a precise technical repair list.
- **Player-variable:** shelter use, conversations, meals and inventory actions.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Tower ở trạng thái nào khiến Link chưa launch ngay?
2. Emergency Shelter cung cấp checkpoint gì mà không buộc một meal cụ thể?
3. Nêu hai hành động player-variable có thể xảy ra trong khoảng chuẩn bị.

## Beat 3 — The Skyview Tower network activates across Hyrule

**Xem:** `03:19:00–03:20:30`

### Mạch story

Link meets Purah at the local Skyview Tower after the preparation interval. The scene widens from one tower to a kingdom-scale activation. Bright flares rise from towers across Hyrule, showing that the network is coming online. The multiple distant signals are the crucial cue: this is not only a local lamp or a single terminal interaction.

Purah reacts with excitement and moves into the operational explanation. The network's activation establishes the infrastructure required for regional map surveys. It does not mean every map region is already revealed on Link's Purah Pad. Each tower still needs a local launch/scan loop, and the current beat stops before Link performs that loop at Lookout Landing.

The consequence is system readiness. A previously unfinished project now produces visible output, and the local terminal can be used for the next setup. The scene offers a causal prerequisite—network activation before scan—without claiming the flares themselves upload a complete map or resolve any quest.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình:** Purah and Link near tower; multiple tower flares across the horizon.
- **Scale:** network-wide activation, not map-complete state.
- **Next:** terminal/Travel Point/Paraglider preparation follows.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `activate the tower network`: bật mạng tháp.
- `a flare rises from each tower`: ánh sáng bay lên từ từng tháp.
- Contrast `activate` /ˈæktəˌveɪt/ with `reveal` /rɪˈvil/: bật hệ thống trước, mở dữ liệu sau.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Purah celebrates/confirms that the network is ready and leads Link to local operation.
- **Logic:** active network enables scanning; it does not automatically reveal every local map.
- **Boundary:** tower flares are visual evidence; adapted explanation is not canon dialogue.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Cue nào cho thấy nhiều tower cùng activate?
2. Vì sao activation chưa đồng nghĩa all maps revealed?
3. Điền hai động từ đúng thứ tự: ___ the network, then ___ local map data.

## Beat 4 — Terminal and Paraglider prepare the launch

**Xem:** `03:20:30–03:22:40`

### Mạch story

At the tower, Link interacts with the Purah Pad terminal. The system registers the location as a Travel Point, creating a navigation function distinct from the aerial scan. Purah then gives Link the Paraglider. The item solves a practical risk created by the tower's launch: he will need to control his descent after being sent high into the air.

Three states should stay separate. Registering the Travel Point supports later fast travel. Receiving the Paraglider adds a reusable mobility item. Standing on the launch platform prepares the scan. None of those states alone reveals Surface or Sky map data; that result appears only after the launch sequence in Beat 5.

Purah's reaction moves from instruction to readiness check as Link takes position. The consequence is an equipped and registered player at the start platform. The source supports the exact UI/game terms and visible handoff; the English practice sentence that explains their relationship remains a learner paraphrase.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình/UI:** terminal interaction, Travel Point registration, Paraglider handoff, launch platform.
- **Function split:** Travel Point = return; Paraglider = descent; platform = scan setup.
- **Do not merge:** map reveal belongs to next clock.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `register a Travel Point`: đăng ký điểm dịch chuyển.
- `control the descent`: điều khiển lúc hạ xuống.
- Contrast `receive an item` and `reveal a map`: handoff is preparation, not scan result.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Purah supplies the item needed for safe post-launch movement; Link steps into position.
- **Logic:** anticipated descent explains the Paraglider handoff. Travel Point registration has a different navigation role.
- **Consequence:** launch prerequisites are visibly complete.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Terminal đăng ký exact system state nào?
2. Paraglider giải quyết phần nào của launch loop?
3. Vì sao Travel Point, Paraglider và map reveal phải được kể thành ba chức năng khác nhau?

## Beat 5 — The launch scans Surface and Sky map layers

**Xem:** `03:22:40–03:24:30`

### Mạch story

The Skyview Tower locks Link into the launch system and fires him high above Central Hyrule. During ascent, the Purah Pad receives scan data. The sequence makes scale visible: Link passes rapidly upward, reaches a survey height and sees the landscape from above. This is the first direct `launch from a Skyview Tower` in the main-story lesson sequence.

The scan reveals map data for both the Surface and Sky layers associated with the area. These are distinct map layers in the Purah Pad, not one flat image. The device update is a navigation result, while the flight itself also leaves Link in open air. He then uses the newly received Paraglider to manage the descent rather than falling without control.

The consequence is a working local survey loop: registered tower, aerial scan, revealed map layers and safe return. It improves knowledge of Central Hyrule but does not complete `Crisis at Hyrule Castle` or any regional quest. The map supports the next decisions; it does not make those decisions or solve their problems.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình:** launch chamber, rapid ascent, scan beams/data, high aerial view, Paraglider descent.
- **UI/state:** Surface map and Sky map reveal for the tower region.
- **Boundary:** map reveal is navigation evidence, not quest resolution.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `launch from a Skyview Tower`: được tháp phóng lên.
- `scan two map layers`: quét hai tầng map.
- Contrast `Surface` /ˈsɝfɪs/ and `Sky` /skaɪ/; do not merge them into one layer.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **System reaction:** tower launches and Purah Pad records map data.
- **Player reaction:** Link deploys Paraglider for controlled descent.
- **Logic:** launch enables aerial scan; map update enables planning, not automatic completion.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Hai map layer nào được reveal trong scan?
2. Link dùng item nào sau ascent để kiểm soát descent?
3. Viết một câu nói map helps planning but does not solve Regional Phenomena.

## Beat 6 — Four regional reports open an investigation with a northwest lead

**Xem:** `03:24:30–03:25:50`

### Mạch story

After Link returns from the launch, Purah examines the updated map and turns the survey into a wider assignment. Reports point to unusual phenomena in four regions: Hebra in the northwest, Eldin in the northeast, Gerudo in the southwest and Lanayru in the east. The Adventure Log opens `Regional Phenomena`, giving each region its own destination and problem.

The story emphasizes a lead toward Rito Village in Hebra. Josha's information and the northwest direction make it a strong first route in the narrative flow. Yet the game permits the player to investigate the regions in different orders. “Emphasized first” describes guidance; “required first” would overstate it.

The consequence is an open-world mission structure. Lookout Landing remains the reporting hub, the castle crisis remains unresolved and Link can choose which regional investigation to pursue. The map now supports route planning, but weather, equipment, supplies and travel sequence remain player-variable. This beat begins the regional search; it does not compress four later story arcs into one summary.

### Móc nhận diện

- **Hình/UI:** updated map, four directional reports/markers, `Regional Phenomena` Adventure Log start.
- **Region set:** Hebra/northwest, Eldin/northeast, Gerudo/southwest, Lanayru/east.
- **Priority boundary:** Rito Village lead is emphasized; order remains open.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `unusual regional phenomena`: các hiện tượng bất thường theo vùng.
- `an open investigation order`: thứ tự điều tra mở.
- Contrast singular `phenomenon` /fəˈnɑmənɑn/ and plural `phenomena` /fəˈnɑmənə/.

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Reaction:** Purah converts survey data and reports into four actionable routes.
- **Logic:** regional reports justify investigation; they do not prove one shared cause yet.
- **Player-variable:** first region, route, gear, weather preparation and return timing.

### Tự kiểm tra

1. Ghép đúng bốn regions với bốn directions.
2. Lead nào được nhấn mạnh trước, và vì sao không được gọi là mandatory order?
3. Crisis at Hyrule Castle ở trạng thái nào khi Regional Phenomena begins?
