## Beat 1 — The terminal turns Wind Temple arrival into a five-lock objective

**Xem:** `07:25:40–07:28:20`

### Mạch story

The handoff from the previous lesson is immediate: Link and Tulin are already on the Stormwind Ark, now identified by the visible title as the Wind Temple. At the central mechanism, the dungeon stops being only a destination and becomes a readable state. The terminal indicates five locks holding the central hatch. That count is a fixed objective, not a suggested estimate.

The visual layout and secondary dungeon source place the locks across 1F, B1 and B2. That distribution tells the learner why a single straight corridor cannot complete the task. It does not, however, prescribe one universal sequence. The player can inspect the map, choose a reachable branch and return when a door, ice block or elevation change suggests a different approach.

Tulin reacts with readiness to help rather than solving the map for Link. His gust is the shared ability required at each turbine, while navigation remains Link's responsibility. The consequence is a two-layer plan: track five objective indicators, then solve one local access problem at a time. Beat 2 follows the first visible branch and demonstrates the gust-to-turbine confirmation loop without declaring it the canonical first lock.

### Móc nhận diện

- **UI/objective:** central mechanism displays five lock indicators.
- **Place:** Wind Temple/Stormwind Ark deck at the storm's center.
- **Boundary:** count is fixed; chosen branch and first target are not.

### Từ vựng trong cảnh

- `five locks hold the hatch`: năm lock giữ central hatch
- `inspect the floor map`: đọc floor distribution trước khi chọn route

### Phản ứng và logic

- **Phản ứng:** Tulin stays available as the wind partner while Link converts the terminal state into a trackable plan.
- **Quan hệ logic:** Because the hatch remains sealed by five indicators, progress requires five local turbine confirmations before a boss entrance can open.
