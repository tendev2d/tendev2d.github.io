### Test 0 — Truy hồi có ranh giới

### Test A — Contact chain

### Test B — Character change with proof

Viết 100 từ giải thích Tulin's change qua three observations: rushed ahead, lost/recovered Swallow Bow, accepted coordinated action. Không dùng adjective chung nếu không gắn action.

### Test C — Gust/glide state machine

### Test D — Sighting boundary

### Test F — Threshold retell

Kể eight sentences trong 60–75 giây. Câu cuối phải chứa both `arrive at the Wind Temple` và `do not complete the dungeon yet`.

## Đáp án

### Đáp án Test 0

Bốn cụm là retrieval ngoài Tulin scene sequence. Island-chain movement relies on companion gust, paraglider, ships/platforms and chosen routes—not an imported tower/rock/cloud/Depths action.

### Đáp án Test A

Teba/Saki identify village crisis and point to Harth; Harth names cave team; Gesane/team reports Talonto direction; Talonto encounter locates Tulin and the Swallow Bow incident.

### Đáp án Test B

Đạt khi each claim has evidence: solo departure/report, item loss/recovery requiring help, then joint combat/ascent. Không đủ nếu chỉ viết “Tulin becomes nicer.”

### Đáp án Test C

Correct state order is target → alignment → gust → glider → landing verification. Missed landing, stamina refill or alternate platform can be recovery; source does not fix one recovery.

### Đáp án Test D

Use: “They observe/follow a figure resembling Zelda”; “the sighting draws them upward”; “the figure may connect to the storm, but identity and cause remain unconfirmed.”

### Đáp án Test E

Severe cold/protection requirement is a fixed environment state. Which armor, meal or elixir solves it depends on save. Stamina, platform order and shrine visit are route variables rather than story prerequisites unless current UI demands them.

### Đáp án Test F

Đạt khi contact chain, bow/teamwork, two-stage ascent, cold gate and arrival boundary are intact. Interior lock count and boss cannot appear as completed states.

### Đáp án tự kiểm tra từng beat

- **Beat 1 —** blizzard harms travel/food supply; conflict concerns coordination/judgment; Harth is verified contact.
- **Beat 2 —** gifted archer plus rushes ahead; cave team next; “Link supports Tulin's investigation without replacing his skill.”
- **Beat 3 —** Gesane/team; initiative created separation; Talonto fixed while cave path/combat/time vary.
- **Beat 4 —** Aerocuda/Swallow Bow; accepting help then partnering; say `figure resembling Zelda, identity unconfirmed`.
- **Beat 5 —** locate target, align, gust, glide, land; ships are route structures; stamina/landing line vary.
- **Beat 6 —** altitude/cloud/gap cues; storm is heading not exact path; check safe landing and available stamina.
- **Beat 7 —** severe-cold warning/current protection; arrival does not equal readiness; armor, meal, elixir vary.
- **Beat 8 —** `Wind Temple`; locks/turbines/boss unresolved; “They arrive at Wind Temple but do not complete it.”
