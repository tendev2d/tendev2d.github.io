- **Vai trò ending:** Mineru farewell is observed on this save and connected narrowly to the Fifth Sage path, not to a broad all-content requirement.

## Đáp án

### Đáp án Test 0

1. The completed save supplies five sages for five revived bosses.
2. The army ends before Link reaches Ganondorf.
3. Ganondorf restores his body during the introduction.
4. Main21 begins at `22:15:20` with the first health bar.

### Đáp án Test A

1. The first is a direct duel; the second adds four phantoms, sages and extended pressure.
2. Four Phantom Ganons appear.
3. Ganondorf knocks the sages out during the latter bodily phase.

### Đáp án Test B

1. Gloom can temporarily lock maximum hearts in this battle.
2. Examples include shield timing, dodge, meals or distance.
3. It triggers Secret Stone escalation and another form.

### Đáp án Test C

1. Ganondorf swallows the Secret Stone.
2. Irreversible draconification sacrifices his self.
3. It creates an aerial dragon battle with body targets.

### Đáp án Test D

1. There are four body weak points.
2. The forehead Secret Stone is final.
3. BeardBear already has the sword; another final sequence may provide it.

### Đáp án Test E

1. Rauru and Sonia join Link.
2. Zelda becomes human and Link regains his original right arm.
3. Human Zelda is still unconscious and falling until Link catches her.

### Đáp án Test F

1. Credits are `22:38:00–22:48:00`.
2. The Fifth Sage path and Mineru reawakening support the farewell.
3. Do not require all Main Quests, all memories, or call it a true ending.

### Đáp án tự kiểm tra từng beat

- **Beat 1 — The Menace Unleashed begins as a direct duel with a player-shaped execution:** The subtitle is The Menace Unleashed. / Gloom hits can temporarily remove maximum hearts. / Other loadouts and defensive timings can reach the same phase transition.
- **Beat 2 — Master of the Secret Stone multiplies the threat before the sages are knocked out:** Four Phantom Ganons appear. / The five sages join the battle. / Ganondorf knocks the sages out during the later part of the form.
- **Beat 3 — Ganondorf chooses irreversible draconification rather than accept bodily defeat:** He swallows his Secret Stone. / He permanently loses his individual self in the dragon form. / The fight moves into open-sky aerial traversal.
- **Beat 4 — The Light Dragon supports a four-point aerial assault and the final forehead strike:** Four body weak points are cleared first. / It catches Link and lifts him above the Demon Dragon. / The final sequence can provide the sword if the player arrived without it.
- **Beat 5 — Rauru and Sonia restore Zelda while Link regains his original arm:** Rauru and Sonia join Link. / Zelda regains human form and Link regains his original right arm. / Zelda is unconscious and falling, so physical rescue remains unfinished.
- **Beat 6 — Link catches Zelda and the Surface reunion resolves the search objective:** It reverses Link's failed attempt to catch Zelda in the prologue. / She lands in water, wakes and returns with Link. / Destroy Ganondorf and Find Princess Zelda are resolved.
- **Beat 7 — A save-conditioned farewell lets Mineru entrust the future to the living sages:** Credits from `22:38:00–22:48:00` remain separate. / Find the Fifth Sage and Mineru's reawakening support the scene. / It rejects all-main-quests and all-memories requirements, among others.
