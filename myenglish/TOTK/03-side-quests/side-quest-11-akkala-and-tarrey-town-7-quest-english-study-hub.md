### Mạch nhiệm vụ, phản ứng và quan hệ nguyên nhân

After Mattison leaves for Gerudo Town, Rhondson reopens the Dream Home service.

Link pays 1,500 Rupees for the plot and two initial rooms, then meets Grantéson southeast of town.

The rooms may be arranged in different valid layouts, but they must stay within the boundary and pass inspection.

The quest establishes Link’s House and awards Hudson Construction Fabric; later room purchases are separate.
