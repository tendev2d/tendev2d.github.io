## Quest 1/5 — A Picture for East Akkala Stable

- **Exact Adventure Log title:** `[EXACT UI] A Picture for East Akkala Stable`.
- **Nguồn của nhiệm vụ:** Arbuckle `HDiN2bNE-Wc`, `01:19`; các mốc `00:00 / 00:28 / 01:01` do uploader công bố giúp tìm cảnh bắt đầu, chụp và nộp ảnh.
- **Giới hạn bằng chứng:** Adventure Log requires the lake when water rises into Octorok shape. Route, hillside angle and how long rain takes remain open.

### Mạch nhiệm vụ, phản ứng và quan hệ nguyên nhân

Rudi wants a painting of Octorok Lake at the moment its outline resembles an Octorok. The request is about the lake's changing shape, not merely its location south of the stable.

Rain raises the water level and fills the missing parts of the outline. A dry-weather picture does not show the requested subject state.

A clear overlook makes the changing lake shape easy to frame. That viewpoint works well, but it is not the only possible camera position.

Rudi copies the picture and gives a Meat and Rice Bowl plus one Pony Point. The food reward belongs to this stable; it should not migrate to another picture quest.

Ozunda chooses the Daruk sculpture that overlooks Goron City because travelers pass Foothill Stable on the way to Eldin. The subject is the sculpture, not any generic Goron statue.

The sculpture belongs to the Goron Stone Memorial. A clear photograph must retain enough of Daruk's figure for the Camera and Ozunda to recognize it.

A nearby shrine and one camera angle work in the video. Neither the fast-travel point nor the exact standing spot appears as an Adventure Log requirement.

After Ozunda copies the photo, he gives a Fireproof Elixir and a Pony Point. The visible reward confirms Elixir, so a stray secondary prose mention of a lizard does not replace it.
