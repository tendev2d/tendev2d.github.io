- **Exact Adventure Log title:** `[EXACT UI] An Uninvited Guest`.

- **Điều kiện xuất hiện:** Speak with Ami outside Wetland Stable when the quest appears; no separate story completion is required.

### Mạch nhiệm vụ, phản ứng và quan hệ nguyên nhân

Ami fears the monsters behind Wetland Stable, but only the Moblin’s defeat advances the quest.

Lizalfos may remain nearby. Defeating every enemy can make the area safer, yet the quest does not require a full camp clear.

After the Moblin falls, Link reports to Ami. Link may choose the weapon, approach and attack order.

Ami gives two Pony Points rather than Rupees or food.

## Đáp án

### Đáp án tự kiểm tra từng quest

- **Quest 1 — An Uninvited Guest:** (1) The Moblin. (2) No; the extra monsters are optional for quest state. (3) Two Pony Points.
- **Quest 2 — Feathered Fugitives:** (1) Gourmets Gone Missing. (2) All five. (3) No; the order is open.
- **Quest 3 — Horse-Drawn Dreams:** (1) Two missing wheels and a caught horse. (2) No; build details and horse choice vary. (3) Zumi.
- **Quest 4 — Piaffe, Packed Away:** (1) None; all clutter must be cleared. (2) No; the stable remains officially shut. (3) One Pony Point.
- **Quest 5 — The Horse Guard's Request:** (1) A Stalhorse from Sanidin Park Ruins at night. (2) No; it is one example of a valid non-horse animal, and a bear also works. (3) Purple Rupee + Pony Point, then Silver Rupee + Pony Point.

### Đáp án Tests 0–F

- **Test 0:** Closed I and II; Forgotten Sword; clear-weather forecast; Chilly Mushroom Risotto.
- **Test A:** Moblin; five Cuccos.
- **Test B:** Two wheels + caught horse; cleanup tidies the building but the stable remains shut.
- **Test C:** Stalhorse → Purple+Point → non-horse animal → Silver+Point.
- **Test D:** Feathered Fugitives; Piaffe, Packed Away.
- **Test E:** Only Moblin controls the first quest; deer is one valid non-horse example, not the only species.
- **Test F:** A correct answer keeps quest-specific counts/rewards and separates the required result from combat, building and taming choices.
