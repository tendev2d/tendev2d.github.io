- **Exact Adventure Log title:** `[EXACT UI] Fish for Fletching`.

- **Điều kiện xuất hiện:** Complete Tulin of Rito Village / restore the Wind Temple region.

### Mạch nhiệm vụ, phản ứng và quan hệ nguyên nhân

Bedoli’s first quest exchange requires exactly three Glowing Cave Fish and awards ten Arrows.

After completion, Bedoli may trade ten Arrows for three Glowing Cave Fish, three Chillfin Trout, or five Brightcaps. That repeat menu is separate from the initial quest.

Link may choose where and in what order to collect the fish. The first quest must not be rewritten as an any-material trade.

The first reward is ten Arrows; repeat exchanges also yield arrows but do not reopen the quest.

### Mạch nhiệm vụ, phản ứng và quan hệ nguyên nhân

Tulin’s clue names Sturnida, Goflam’s and Sherfin’s secret springs and points to their center.

North Biron Snowshelf Cave contains three correct cracked walls. Opening them channels water into the chamber and raises the central structure.

Link may choose the cave path and avoid or fight enemies; only the three water channels must reach the center.

The chest contains the Vah Medoh Divine Helm. If Link already owns it, the quest may close as soon as Tulin gives the request.
