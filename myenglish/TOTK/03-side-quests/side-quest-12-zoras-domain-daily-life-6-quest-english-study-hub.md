## Quest 1/6 — A Crabulous Deal

- **Exact Adventure Log title:** `[EXACT UI] A Crabulous Deal`.
- **Nguồn của nhiệm vụ:** [Tears Of The Kingdom | A Crabulous Deal - Side Quest | Walkthrough Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5AvUA_LWP8A), uploader **Live Gaming Paradise**, thời lượng `04:27`; dùng toàn khoảng `00:00–04:27` để đối chiếu hình ảnh.
- **Giới hạn:** Lần đầu Cleff cần đúng 10 Bright-Eyed Crabs và trao một Sapphire; các lần đổi lặp lại về sau là giao dịch riêng.
- **Điều kiện xuất hiện:** Chỉ mở sau khi hoàn tất Sidon of the Zora.

### Mạch nhiệm vụ, phản ứng và quan hệ nguyên nhân

After Sidon of the Zora, Cleff asks Link for ten Bright-Eyed Crabs.

The first request still needs exactly ten, even though Link may collect the crabs by any route.

Cleff gives one Sapphire when the initial ten are handed over.

Any later crab exchange belongs to the repeatable trade and must not replace the first quest reward.

## Đáp án

### Đáp án tự kiểm tra từng quest

- **Quest 1 — A Crabulous Deal:** (1) Sidon of the Zora. (2) Ten Bright-Eyed Crabs. (3) They are repeatable trades; the quest reward is one Sapphire.
- **Quest 2 — A Token of Friendship:** (1) Behind a waterfall in the drained Ancient Zora Waterworks. (2) No. (3) Sidon of the Zora must be complete.
- **Quest 3 — A Wife Wafted Away:** (1) On Wellspring Island. (2) No; Link speaks with her and reports back. (3) Report to Fronk, then receive a Hearty Fish Skewer.
- **Quest 4 — Mired in Muck:** (1) Yes. (2) No. (3) The quest may complete automatically and award the Zora Spear.
- **Quest 5 — The Blue Stone:** (1) One. (2) Reservoir Lakefront Cavern. (3) No; it is an optional transport method.
- **Quest 6 — True Treasure:** (1) Kodah in the Domain or Sasan in Tarm Point Cave. (2) It must bring Sasan safely to Finley across the fluctuating water. (3) One from Sasan and one from Finley.

### Đáp án Tests 0–F

- **Test 0:** Prototype under3:00; race under2:00. Lynel horns pay 50/100/300, not one Purple reward.
- **Test A:** Cleff—Crabulous; Yona—Token; Fronk—Wife; Bazz—Mired; Ledo—Blue Stone; Kodah—True Treasure. Sasan can also start True Treasure.
- **Test B:** 10 Bright-Eyed Crabs; 1 Blue Stone; 2 Opals. Rewards are Sapphire, Greaves, Skewer, Spear, Fabric and 2 Opals.
- **Test C:** All except Mired are post-Sidon. Mired can start before and auto-complete after the muck clears.
- **Test D:** Talus is not needed for the chest; raft is one transport choice. True Treasure must bring Sasan to Finley.
- **Test E:** Videos confirm English UI, visible actions and results. Guide narration/ASR is not NPC subtitle.
- **Test F:** The retell preserves first trade, five post-Sidon gates, Mired exception, open methods and two Opals.
