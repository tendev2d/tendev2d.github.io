## Đáp án

### Đáp án tự kiểm tra từng quest

- **Quest 1 — A Bottled Cry for Help:** (1) Yes. (2) A rock in Mapla Point Cave, moved with Ultrahand. (3) No; it is one useful approach.
- **Quest 2 — A New Champion's Tunic:** (1) Hyrule Castle Sanctum. (2) Two. (3) Champion’s Leathers.
- **Quest 3 — Dantz's Prize Cows:** (1) Three Acorns for one Fresh Milk. (2) No; the same exchange remains afterward. (3) Yes.
- **Quest 4 — Homegrown in Hateno:** (1) The Mayoral Election. (2) At daybreak. (3) East Wind begins stocking Sun Pumpkins.
- **Quest 5 — Manny's Beloved:** (1) Ivee. (2) Ten Hot-Footed Frogs to Manny. (3) Ten Rushrooms.
- **Quest 6 — Photographing a Chuchu:** (1) Paraglider, Camera, an alternate fabric, and one paid 20-Rupee fabric change. (2) A common blue Chuchu. (3) No.
- **Quest 7 — Teach Me a Lesson I:** (1) The Screen Depicting the Calamity upstairs in Paya Hall. (2) During the class session, commonly 9:00–14:00. (3) No; keep it `[CONFLICT]/Unknown`.
- **Quest 8 — Teach Me a Lesson II:** (1) Teach Me a Lesson I. (2) Hylian Rice, Goron Spice and Monster Extract. (3) Access to the School’s Field.
- **Quest 9 — Uma's Garden:** (1) Teach Me a Lesson I and II. (2) About one real gameplay hour outside Hateno; sleep, campfire and time-skip do not advance it. (3) Nine normally, three Endura Carrots, and two for either radish.

### Đáp án Tests 0–F

- **Test 0:** Codgers+Out opened two slabs; Codgers opened Follow; Moza charges 5 first and 10 later; narrator ASR was not NPC subtitle.
