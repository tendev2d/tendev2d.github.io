## Quest 1/6 — A Way to Trade, Washed Away

- **Exact Adventure Log title:** `[EXACT UI] A Way to Trade, Washed Away`.
- **Nguồn của nhiệm vụ:** [A Way To Trade, Washed Away Side Quest Guide in Zelda Tears of the Kingdom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pmOmbriJtuk), uploader **Quick Tips**, thời lượng `03:31`; dùng toàn khoảng `00:00–03:31` để đối chiếu hình ảnh.
- **Giới hạn:** Video chỉ minh họa một cách ghép phương tiện. Mục tiêu chỉ khóa một trong ba thuyền ở Tenoko và bến Lurelin.
- **Điều kiện xuất hiện:** Ruffian-Infested Village và Lurelin Village Restoration Project là hai điều kiện được các nguồn cùng xác nhận. Riêng Village Attacked by Pirates có thông tin không thống nhất, nên giữ ở `[CONFLICT]/Unknown`.

### Mạch nhiệm vụ, phản ứng và quan hệ nguyên nhân

After Ruffian-Infested Village and Lurelin Village Restoration Project, Garini needs one replacement boat to reopen the general store.

Three suitable boats remain near Tenoko Island; Link may choose any one and build any workable towing or propulsion setup.

Sources disagree on whether Village Attacked by Pirates is a separately required condition or an automatically resolved part of the same restoration chain, so that narrower claim remains `[CONFLICT]/Unknown`.

## Đáp án

### Đáp án tự kiểm tra từng quest

- **Quest 1 — A Way to Trade, Washed Away:** (1) Any of three boats; deliver one. (2) Boat choice, build and sea route. (3) `[CONFLICT]/Unknown`, not a separately required prerequisite.
- **Quest 2 — Dad's Blue Shirt:** (1) Lurelin Village Restoration Project. (2) The iron chest is fixed; raft, plank and route are optional. (3) No; Sebasto lets Link keep it.
- **Quest 3 — Lurelin Resort Project:** (1) Lurelin Restoration opens it; Master the Vehicle Prototype and Tarrey Town Race is On are additionally required. (2) Under 2:00 with the special Steering Stick, while hitting the required lights. (3) Free Lurelin Water Rally access.
- **Quest 4 — Ousting the Giants:** (1) Corta Lake Cave, Rodai Lakefront Tunnel and Calora Lake Cave. (2) No. (3) Mighty Fried Bananas.
- **Quest 5 — Rattled Ralera:** (1) Mighty Porgy or Armored Porgy. (2) Hylian Rice and Goron Spice. (3) No; any valid gust method can work.
- **Quest 6 — Seeking the Pirate Hideout:** (1) Three. (2) Report the three cleared surface groups to Sesami. (3) Clear the pirate-ship cave group, then report to Sesami again.

### Đáp án Tests 0–F

- **Test 0:** Teach I+II; Sayge needed a saved Album photo; Homegrown ran night→daybreak; Uma used about one real hour outside Hateno.
- **Test A:** Bring one of three Tenoko boats; whether Village Attacked is a separate prerequisite remains unknown. Dad’s iron chest is fixed, travel tools optional.
- **Test B:** Restoration + Master the Vehicle Prototype + Tarrey Town Race is On; use special Steering Stick, hit lights, finish under 2:00.
- **Test C:** Corta Lake Cave, Rodai Lakefront Tunnel, Calora Lake Cave; order and loadout are open.
- **Test D:** Mighty or Armored Porgy + Rice + Goron Spice, then any valid gust.
- **Test E:** Clear 3 surface groups any order→report→clear cave ship group→report. The report opens the next stage.
- **Test F:** A correct briefing preserves all six titles, prerequisites, counts, required sequence and rewards without fixing optional methods.
