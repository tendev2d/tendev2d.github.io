- **Nguồn của nhiệm vụ:** [Kiranico — Dalia's Game](https://zelda.kiranico.com/totk/data/quests/FindSunaNui) dùng cho title/trạng thái đã trích xuất; [Zelda Dungeon — Dalia's Game](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Dalia%27s_Game) dùng để kiểm chéo điều kiện, bước làm và phần thưởng.

Dalia turns access to one heroine orb into a timed hide-and-seek game inside Gerudo Shelter.

Finding the toy before time expires convinces Dalia to let Link use the recovered orb for the heroine-statue chain.

Later fruit rewards belong to replaying the game; they are not the first quest result.

Returning all seven small orbs opens a hidden room containing the giant orb; individual placements can yield Hydromelons.

The giant orb belongs at the sanctuary entrance beneath North Gerudo Ruins, where a stela identifies the eighth hero as a Hylian voe.
