## Quest 1/5 — A Picture for New Serenne Stable

- **Exact Adventure Log title:** `[EXACT UI] A Picture for New Serenne Stable`.

### Mạch nhiệm vụ, phản ứng và quan hệ nguyên nhân

Sprinn wants a local picture that can become a wall painting. The requested subject class is a geoglyph rather than one named geoglyph.

The nearby ground pattern can be framed clearly from above for Camera recognition. Its convenience does not create a mandatory route or viewpoint.

Sprinn accepts the saved image and copies it for display. Travel device, altitude and exact framing remain choices for the player.

The turn-in awards Bright Tomato Mushroom Stew and one Pony Point. That reward belongs to New Serenne Stable, not to the other four cards.
