## Quest 1/7 — Goddess Statue of Courage

- **Exact Adventure Log title:** `[EXACT UI] Goddess Statue of Courage`.
- **Nguồn của nhiệm vụ:** [Zelda Dungeon — Goddess Statue of Courage](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Goddess_Statue_of_Courage) dùng để đối chiếu điều kiện, mục tiêu và phần thưởng; đây là hướng dẫn cộng đồng, không phải bản chép lời trong game.
- **Giới hạn:** Farosh đi theo loop không gắn một giờ cố định tại một vị trí; phải lấy đúng Claw bằng cách đánh trúng limb.
- **Điều kiện xuất hiện:** Cầu nguyện tại Goddess Statue ở Spring of Courage; không cần hoàn tất Power hoặc Wisdom trước.

### Mạch nhiệm vụ, phản ứng và quan hệ nguyên nhân

Prayer at the Spring of Courage begins the request because its statue cannot sense the Mother Goddess Statue.

If Link has not already examined the toppled Mother in the Forgotten Temple, he does so and reports the finding.

The Courage statue then asks for Farosh’s Claw. Farosh follows a loop, so the quest does not promise one fixed hour at one spot.

Dropping the claw in the spring sends power to the Mother and gives a Topaz.

| Result | Courage sends power toward the Mother Goddess Statue. | một trong ba nguồn sức mạnh |
| Reward | Topaz. | Courage phần thưởng |
| Phụ thuộc người chơi | Find Farosh anywhere along its loop and choose how to reach the spring. | no cố định location hour |

## Đáp án

### Đáp án tự kiểm tra từng quest

- **Quest 1 — Goddess Statue of Courage:** (1) Farosh’s Claw. (2) No; Farosh travels a loop. (3) A Topaz.
- **Quest 2 — Goddess Statue of Power:** (1) Dinraal and Dinraal’s Claw. (2) Yes; no single location hour is required. (3) A Ruby.
- **Quest 3 — Goddess Statue of Wisdom:** (1) Naydra’s Claw. (2) No; Naydra travels a loop. (3) A Sapphire.
- **Quest 4 — The Mother Goddess Statue:** (1) Goddess Statue of Courage, Power and Wisdom. (2) No; all three are required, but their order is open. (3) The White Sword of the Sky.
- **Quest 5 — Eldin's Colossal Fossil:** (1) Near East Akkala Stable. (2) The missing fossilized eye in the empty socket. (3) Yes; the restoration state, not one tool, defines success.
- **Quest 6 — Hebra's Colossal Fossil:** (1) Eldin’s Colossal Fossil. (2) A fin bone and a spine/vertebral bone. (3) No; the freeing methods may vary.
- **Quest 7 — Gerudo's Colossal Fossil:** (1) Hebra’s Colossal Fossil. (2) The child torso and tail. (3) Yes; the completed arrangement is fixed, not one tool.

### Đáp án Tests 0–F

- **Test 0:** The Fierce Deity; Twins Manuscript; the dog only points to the genuine chest.
- **Test A:** Farosh→Courage, Dinraal→Power, Naydra→Wisdom; their exact parts are Claws.
- **Test B:** Courage→Topaz, Power→Ruby, Wisdom→Sapphire; all three may be completed in any order, then Mother gives White Sword.
- **Test C:** Eldin→Hebra→Gerudo, each Purple Rupee/50; each completion sends Loone to the next fossil.
- **Test D:** Eldin eye; Hebra fin+spine; Gerudo child torso+tail. Tools and alignment motions may vary.
- **Test E:** Dragons loop without one fixed hour; bombs/Yunobo for Eldin and different rubble/ice tools at Hebra are examples.
