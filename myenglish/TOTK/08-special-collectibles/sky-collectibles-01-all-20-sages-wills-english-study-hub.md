## Khung câu dùng khi tra ledger

Dùng các khung `[PARA]` để tra row, guard split và exchange effect. Tên boss trong ledger không biến câu mẫu thành lời combat nguyên văn.

| Khung | Câu mẫu của bài | Chức năng |
|---:|---|---|
| `A is guarded by B, while C has no boss listed.` | Wellspring Island is guarded by Flux Construct II, while Sokkala has no boss listed. | so guard field |
| `There are A plus B guarded rows, for C total.` | There are five Flux plus three King Gleeok rows, for eight total. | nói split |
| `Four Wills strengthen one A; twenty Wills strengthen B.` | Four Wills strengthen one Solemn Vow; twenty Wills strengthen all five once. | nói exchange math |
| `The upgrade increases A, not B or C.` | The upgrade increases avatar attack, not Link attack or cooldown. | khóa effect |
