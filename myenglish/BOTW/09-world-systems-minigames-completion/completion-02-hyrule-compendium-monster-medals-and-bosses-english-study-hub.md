## Hai sổ hệ thống — Compendium workflow và Medal counts

### Hyrule Compendium workflow

| Stage | System behavior |
|---|---|
| Restore the Camera | Complete the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab portion of `Locked Mementos`. |
| Photograph a subject | Frame a valid creature, monster, material, weapon, bow, shield, or treasure target until its name registers. |
| Upgrade the Sensor | Complete `Slated for Upgrades` and give Purah **3 Ancient Screws** to receive `Sheikah Sensor +`. |
| Learn targeting and unlock Symin's service | Complete `Sunshroom Sensing`; it teaches targeting a registered entry and unlocks Symin's picture-purchase service. |
| Buy ordinary pictures | Symin sells a random picture from a selected ordinary category for **100 Rupees** after his service unlocks. |
| Obtain starred-clear access | Defeat Ganon once so the save bears a star; prior eligible overworld-boss kills still count for medals. |
| Buy Elite Enemy pictures | After the starred clear, Symin sells Elite Enemy pictures for **500 Rupees** each. |
| Complete all 385 | Fill every base Normal Mode, no-DLC entry and receive the `Classified Envelope` from Symin. |

### Denominator theo mode và DLC

| Mode / installed content | Compendium total | Cách dùng trong bài |
|---|---|---|
| Normal Mode · base game · no DLC | **385** | mẫu số chính của bài |
| Normal Mode · DLC2 installed | **389** | bốn entries DLC2 được thêm |
| Master Mode · without the four DLC2 Compendium additions | **390** | không trộn với Normal Mode |
| Master Mode · DLC2 entries included | **394** | version/mode riêng |

### Medal of Honor ledger

| Medal | Required unique base-game encounters | Included family | Important exclusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Medal of Honor: Hinox` | 40 | Hinox variants and Stalnox locations | Ordinary Moblins/Bokoblins; repeated respawns |
| `Medal of Honor: Talus` | 40 | Stone, Frost, and Igneo Talus locations | Pebblits; repeated respawns |
| `Medal of Honor: Molduga` | 4 | The four base Gerudo Desert Moldugas | Other desert enemies |

`★ Defeated` cho biết một eligible boss location đã được hạ trên starred save. Kilton báo **number slain so far** và trao medal khi category đạt 40/40/4; repeated Blood Moon respawns ở cùng location không thay một instance còn thiếu.
